🧭 Candidate sources (not yet integrated)

These official European datasets are evaluated for integration but are not currently used — HydraShield does not claim their capabilities:

  • EFFIS / GWIS (Copernicus EMS, JRC) — European fire-danger maps & burned-area perimeters; currently accessible as a view-only WMS, under evaluation for reference comparison
  • Copernicus Sentinel-1 — SAR moisture under cloud cover
  • Copernicus Sentinel-3 — land surface temperature
  • Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem — direct L2A access (currently via the public Earth Search STAC)

🔍 Provenance by design

Every analysis component is labelled observed / derived / modelled / forecast / unavailable with acquisition time, resolution and limitations.

  • No simulated or randomised values
  • Unavailable sources are stated, never filled in

AI & Modelling

Advanced Prediction

Combining machine learning with physics-based models for accurate fire prediction.

🧠 Machine Learning

ML models trained on historical wildfire data to identify risk patterns and predict fire behavior.

  • Risk assessment models
  • Fuel moisture prediction
  • Fire spread probability
  • Pattern recognition

🔬 Physics-Based Models

Established landscape-scale wildfire spread models integrated with soil physics for accurate simulation.

  • Fire propagation modelling
  • Soil-to-fuel moisture transfer
  • Capillary rise simulation
  • Vadose-zone dynamics

💧 Soil Physics

Modelling capillary moisture transfer in the vadose zone to predict fuel moisture content dynamics.

  • Soil moisture retention curves
  • Hydraulic conductivity
  • Evapotranspiration modelling
  • Subsurface water movement

🛡️ Digital Twin

A simulated environment that allows for scenario testing and dynamic protection-zone optimization.

  • Real-time simulation
  • Scenario testing
  • Protection zone optimization
  • Water resource management
