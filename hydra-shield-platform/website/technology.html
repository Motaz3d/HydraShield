Technology Stack

Our Data Sources

HydraShield integrates multiple European Earth Observation and climate data sources.

🛰️ Copernicus Sentinel-1

Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) providing cloud-free monitoring capabilities, critical for continuous observation during fire events.

  • All-weather, day/night imaging
  • Soil moisture estimation
  • Cloud cover mitigation
  • 6-day revisit cycle

🛰️ Copernicus Sentinel-2

High-resolution multispectral imaging for vegetation health, fuel load assessment, and land cover classification.

  • 10m spatial resolution
  • 13 spectral bands
  • Vegetation indices (NDVI, NDWI)
  • 5-day revisit cycle

🛰️ Copernicus Sentinel-3

Medium-resolution optical and thermal imaging for large-scale fire detection and land surface temperature monitoring.

  • Thermal infrared fire detection
  • Land surface temperature
  • Global coverage
  • Daily revisit

🌤️ ECMWF Weather Data

European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts providing critical meteorological inputs for fire spread prediction.

  • Wind speed and direction
  • Temperature and humidity
  • Precipitation forecasts
  • ERA5-Land soil moisture reanalysis
AI & Modelling

Advanced Prediction

Combining machine learning with physics-based models for accurate fire prediction.

🧠 Machine Learning

ML models trained on historical wildfire data to identify risk patterns and predict fire behavior.

  • Risk assessment models
  • Fuel moisture prediction
  • Fire spread probability
  • Pattern recognition

🔬 Physics-Based Models

Established landscape-scale wildfire spread models integrated with soil physics for accurate simulation.

  • Fire propagation modelling
  • Soil-to-fuel moisture transfer
  • Capillary rise simulation
  • Vadose-zone dynamics

💧 Soil Physics

Modelling capillary moisture transfer in the vadose zone to predict fuel moisture content dynamics.

  • Soil moisture retention curves
  • Hydraulic conductivity
  • Evapotranspiration modelling
  • Subsurface water movement

🛡️ Digital Twin

A simulated environment that allows for scenario testing and dynamic protection-zone optimization.

  • Real-time simulation
  • Scenario testing
  • Protection zone optimization
  • Water resource management
