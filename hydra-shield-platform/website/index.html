The Challenge

Wildfires Are Becoming Unstoppable

Climate change is intensifying wildfire frequency, severity, and unpredictability across the globe.

🔥

Reactive Firefighting

Conventional firefighting is strictly reactive, attempting to suppress active fires with massive, inefficient water volumes.

💧

80% Water Loss

Aerial drops lose up to 80% of water to evaporation, drift, and runoff — costing €20,000 per hour.

🗺️

Static Risk Maps

Existing prediction software stops at risk-mapping, leaving agencies without actionable execution plans.

🏘️

Vulnerable Communities

Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) communities face catastrophic risk with limited evacuation options.

AI-Driven Digital Twin for Wildfire Risk Mitigation

We integrate Copernicus satellite data (Sentinel-1/2/3) with climate models and soil physics to predict dynamic moisture transfer — moving beyond static risk maps to real-time, predictive fire-spread modelling.

  • ✅ Dynamic Critical Protection Zone calculation
  • ✅ Water-optimized intervention planning
  • ✅ Subsurface hydration strategy
  • ✅ Evacuation corridor security
