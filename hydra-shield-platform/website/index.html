How it works

Three steps, no black box

You do not have to know the EU vocabulary first — start from your sites.

1
Send your site coordinates
A place name, a lat/lon pair, or a list of sites — up to 25 on the free tier, 100 for subscribers.
2
The engine reads real data
Satellites, reanalysis archives and official registries score each site against up to 10 hazards — wildfire, flood, heat, drought, wind, coastal, cyclone and more.
3
You get a traceable PDF pack
Sources, dates, engine version and an honest “unavailable” where data is missing. Free check first; decision-ready pack €19.
