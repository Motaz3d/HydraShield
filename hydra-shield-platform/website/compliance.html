Who must comply — and when

Regulatory state as of September 2026, after the adopted "stop-the-clock" directive. Items still in proposal (Omnibus scope changes) are labelled as proposals, never as law. This page is evidence support, not legal advice — confirm your position with counsel.

CSRD — Waves 1 & 2

In force

Large public-interest companies report since FY2024. Other large undertakings report on FY2027 (first reports published 2028) after the two-year delay.

CSRD — Waves 3 & 4

FY2028

Listed SMEs and non-EU groups with >€150M EU turnover report on FY2028 (first reports 2029). The Omnibus proposal to remove Wave 3 remains a proposal.

EUDR

Live now

Large operators and traders since 30 Dec 2025; SMEs since 30 June 2026. Geolocation evidence for commodity plots is the core obligation.

CSDDD

2027–2028

Applies from 26 July 2027 (>€1.5B net turnover) and 26 July 2028 (>€900M) — including non-EU companies with EU turnover.

Simplified ESRS

Adopted 3 Jul 2026

Adopted 3 July 2026, pending application. Physical-risk datapoints — hazard screening, scenario analysis, E1-9 anticipated financial effects — remain in scope.

Beyond the EU

ISSB / SB 261

IFRS S2 adoption spreads across jurisdictions; California SB 261 physical-risk reports apply from 1 Jan 2026 for entities above $500M revenue.
