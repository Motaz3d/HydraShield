Historical environmental events

REPORTED

Constraints & risk flags

INFERRED

Site-specific environmental constraints and risk flags derived from the evidence base.

Framework references & maps

DOCUMENTED

References to international frameworks and documentation, plus maps and graphics ready for a permit file.

Evidence taxonomy used throughout

OBSERVED DOCUMENTED REPORTED MODELLED INFERRED UNKNOWN

Every statement is tagged with one of these labels and traceable to its source. Where data is unavailable, the label is UNKNOWN and the gap is declared — never filled in.

How it works

From site definition to delivered dossier

  1. Define the site and the project. Share the location, boundaries and the type of development or investment.
  2. The platform builds the automated evidence base. It cross-matches the site against satellite, land-cover, hazard and historical-event layers.
  3. Expert advisory review. The evidence is reviewed and framed as scientific arguments for an application or authority screening.
  4. Branded PDF dossier delivered. A traceable, reference-ready document with maps, graphics and source notes.

Built on the same verification engine as the platform's Environmental Forensic Verification and Report Builder products.

What this is not. This service is an advisory evidence product, not a legal permit, not an official government decision, and not a substitute for the competent authority's licensing procedure. Final licensing decisions remain with the relevant authorities.
