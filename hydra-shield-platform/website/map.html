Key terms in plain language

Observed vs modelled
Observed = measured (satellite scene, recorded event). Modelled = computed from a model (forecast, projection, scenario). The filter on the left separates the two — they are never mixed silently.
FWI (Fire Weather Index)
A weather-driven index of how easily fire could spread today. It says nothing about ignition or probability of loss.
NDMI / NDVI
Satellite vegetation signals from Sentinel-2. NDMI ≈ fuel moisture (dry vs moist), NDVI ≈ greenness. A 10 m pixel can see a park, not a single tree.
FIRMS
NASA's near-real-time active-fire detections. A hotspot is a heat detection, not a fire perimeter.
ESA WorldCover
A global 10 m land-cover map used to cross-verify land-use claims.
