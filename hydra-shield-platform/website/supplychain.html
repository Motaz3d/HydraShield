Screening evidence only. This is NOT a EUDR compliance verification, NOT a deforestation-free certificate, and NOT a supply-chain audit. Verdicts are partial evidence or no evidence only.

Frameworks & scope

EU Deforestation Regulation

regulatory context

Requires proof of deforestation-free status after 31 Dec 2020. Talaix currently provides only a partial evidence layer.

UK Forest Risk Commodity Scheme

regulatory context

Operator-level due-diligence evidence can be supported, but forest-loss verification is not available.

Green Claims

commercial context

Reports existing evidence and missing evidence. A claim cannot be verified green without a forest-loss time series.

What this is

  • Land-cover snapshot from ESA WorldCover 10m 2021.
  • Recent Sentinel-2 NDVI/NDMI when a cloud-free scene exists.
  • An explicit list of declared data gaps.

What this is not

  • A verified deforestation-free certificate.
  • A verified green claim.
  • A forest-loss time series (GFW/Hansen/RADD not integrated).
  • EUDR compliance or a supply-chain audit.
