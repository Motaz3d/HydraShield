The Challenge

The Growing Wildfire Crisis

Climate change is intensifying wildfire frequency, severity, and unpredictability across the globe, threatening communities, infrastructure, and ecosystems.

🔥 The Escalating Threat

Wildfires are becoming increasingly devastating due to climate change. Rising temperatures, prolonged droughts, and changing precipitation patterns create ideal conditions for catastrophic fire events.

  • Wildfire season now lasts 78 days longer than in the 1970s
  • Annual burned area in Europe has doubled in the last decade
  • Mediterranean regions face the highest risk in Europe
  • WUI (Wildland-Urban Interface) communities are most vulnerable

💧 The Water Crisis

Conventional firefighting is reactive and water-intensive. Aerial firefighting costs approximately €20,000 per hour with up to 80% evaporative water loss.

  • Up to 10,000L per aerial drop
  • 60-80% water lost to evaporation and drift
  • €20,000 per hour for aerial operations
  • Water-scarce regions face critical resource constraints

🗺️ The Intelligence Gap

Existing fire-prediction software stops at risk-mapping, leaving civil protection agencies without actionable, preemptive execution plans.

  • Static risk maps based on outdated vegetation data
  • No dynamic soil-to-fuel moisture transfer modelling
  • No water-optimized intervention planning
  • Limited integration with early warning systems

🏘️ The Human Cost

Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) communities face catastrophic risk with limited evacuation options, especially those with single-access roads.

  • Vulnerable communities with single-access evacuation routes
  • Critical infrastructure at risk (hospitals, schools, power grids)
  • Insurance losses from unmitigated wildfires
  • Growing regulatory requirements for climate adaptation
