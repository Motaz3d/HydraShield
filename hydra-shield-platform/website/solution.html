How It Works

From Satellite to Soil

A 5-layer adaptive closed-loop architecture with a strict Human-in-the-Loop Decision Gate.

🛰️ Earth Observation Layer

Ingests Copernicus Sentinel-2/3, DEM data, and real-time weather (ECMWF/Meteosat). Cloud cover mitigation through Sentinel-1 SAR and ERA5-Land soil moisture reanalysis.

  • Sentinel-1 SAR for cloud-free monitoring
  • Sentinel-2/3 multispectral data
  • ECMWF weather forecasts
  • ERA5-Land soil moisture reanalysis

🧠 Fire Risk & Spread Prediction

Combines Machine Learning with established landscape-scale physics-based wildfire spread models to predict fire propagation with high accuracy.

  • ML-based risk assessment
  • Physics-based fire spread modelling
  • Real-time prediction updates
  • Historical hindcasting validation

💧 Protection Optimisation

Calculates the Critical Protection Zone. In Water-Scarce mode, the AI optimizes resource allocation, prioritizing critical infrastructure and evacuation routes.

  • Dynamic Critical Protection Zone
  • Water-Scarce resource allocation
  • Critical infrastructure prioritization
  • Single-access evacuation route security

🛡️ Adaptive Water Intervention

Determines where, when, and how much water to deploy. Interoperable with existing early-warning systems (e.g., EU CECIS) for synchronized alerts.

  • Subsurface hydration strategy
  • Optimized water deployment
  • Early warning system integration
  • Machine-readable outputs
Human-in-the-Loop

AI Recommendations, Human Decisions

The AI generates a traffic-light recommendation for each protection zone, with a configurable human decision window.

🟢

Green - Deploy

High confidence, low uncertainty → authorized operator approval for immediate deployment.

🟡

Yellow - Review

Moderate uncertainty → operator reviews additional model outputs before deciding.

🔴

Red - Hold

High uncertainty or conflicting data → operator overrides or requests manual reassessment.

⏱️

Decision Window

A configurable human decision window is maintained between model recommendation and physical activation.

Competitive Advantage

Why HydraShield?

✅ Unique Value Proposition

HydraShield provides an Actionable Protection Blueprint that translates EO-derived wildfire risk information into a spatially and temporally optimized water-management strategy.

  • Delivers actionable execution blueprint
  • Dynamically manages water consumption
  • Simulates subsurface-to-surface moisture transfer
  • Operates in Water-Scarce resource allocation modes
  • Interoperable with Civil Protection (Copernicus EMS)
  • Quantifies Water-Use Efficiency Ratio (WUER)

📊 Key Metrics

HydraShield relies on rigorous mathematical foundations to optimize resources and quantify impact.

  • WUER: (Risk_baseline - Risk_HydraShield) / Volume of water applied
  • MEFMI: FMC_target - FMC_baseline
  • P_spread: f(FMC, wind, slope, fuel type)
  • ROS_reduced: ROS_baseline × R_FMC(MEFMI, fuel type, weather, slope)
  • A_critical: min(A) subject to P_spread(t) ≤ θ
