How it works

From Evidence to Action

The same chain runs for every hazard — wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind, coastal exposure and tropical cyclones.

🛰️ Evidence

Every result is computed from real, documented datasets — Earth observation, official open data, historical archives — with source, acquisition time, resolution and limitations attached.

  • Real data only — unavailable is stated, never filled in
  • Claim status: OBSERVED · DOCUMENTED · REPORTED · MODELLED · INFERRED · UNKNOWN
  • Temporal class: OBSERVED · HISTORICAL · FORECAST · PROJECTED · SCENARIO

🌪️ Hazard intelligence

Per-hazard screening levels for eight hazards — current conditions, trends and historical context — computed from the documented datasets behind each hazard.

  • Screening indicators, explicitly not validated predictions
  • Current + historical analysis per place
  • Per-dataset temporal coverage drives every year selector

🏙️ Exposure intelligence

Who and what is in the way — population and settlements, hospitals and schools, buildings, agriculture, transport, energy, tourism and industry — counted from real mapped data.

  • Population, business, infrastructure and critical-service exposure distinguished
  • Completeness caveats stated per block
  • "Who is exposed?" — not only "what is the hazard?"

📊 Economic meaning → action

Structured exposure profiles for finance, insurance and government decisions, and solutions matched to the exact place — with honest non-quantification where no documented basis exists.

  • Exposure before euros — never a fabricated loss figure
  • Nature-based, engineering and technology options with limitations
  • Reports carry ID, engine version and evidence status

Climate-extremes intelligence

Multi-hazard evidence for financial decisions

Talaix's analysis pipeline began with wildfire and now covers seven climate and natural hazards. The same evidence engine serves banks, insurers, investors and governments with traceable hazard, exposure and loss context — no invented numbers, no guaranteed outcomes.

What the platform covers

Wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind, coastal exposure and tropical cyclones — each assessed from documented data with source, date, resolution and limitations attached.

What it is not

Not a validated prediction of losses or disasters, and not a substitute for site-specific due diligence. Hazard levels are screening indicators unless explicitly labelled validated; unavailable data is declared, never filled in.
