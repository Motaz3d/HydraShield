Roadmap

Our Strategic Journey

From digital feasibility to commercial scale-up - a staged approach that reduces risk before significant capital is deployed.

Development Phases

Next 12-24 Months

Currently at TRL 3 (Digital Feasibility). Our strategy focuses on rigorous scientific validation and securing non-dilutive funding.

Phase 1 · Months 1-9

Digital Feasibility & Company Formation

Establish HQ in a strategically selected jurisdiction based on regulatory, tax, and funding criteria. Secure €50k-€100k via EU grants (Copernicus Incubation, CASSINI) and local programs. Deliver a validated Digital Twin Proof of Concept using historical wildfire hindcasting.

Phase 2 · Months 10-18

Field Validation

Establish a User Advisory Board to secure Letters of Intent (LoI). Execute a physical field prototype in a high-risk European WUI zone.

Phase 3 · Months 19-24

European Demonstration

Participate in Horizon Europe/Interreg collaborative projects. Demonstrate across multiple European wildfire-risk environments.

Phase 4 · Beyond 24 Months

Commercial Scale-Up

Finalize IP strategy, package the SaaS offering, and apply for the EIC Accelerator for commercial scale-up.
