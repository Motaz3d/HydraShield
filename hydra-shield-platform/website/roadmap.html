Roadmap

Product Roadmap

From evidence engine to compliance-evidence products — a staged roadmap that prioritises honest, documented data over speculative claims.

Stages

Building the evidence layer for EU climate disclosure

Honest, non-committal timing: capabilities ship when the underlying data and validation are ready; no capability is released with invented figures.

Stage 1 · Live today

Compliance-evidence products on a multi-hazard engine

One evidence base for eight hazards — wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind, coastal exposure, tropical cyclones and earthquakes — powering the compliance products: CSRD/ESRS E1 physical-risk evidence packs with the CsrdTX applicability engine and machine-readable XBRL, EU Taxonomy DNSH verification, EUDR supply-chain screening, insurance risk and environmental forensics — with a free CSRD scope check and published prices.

Stage 2 · In progress

First customers and ESRS E1 depth

The first-customer motion: compliance outreach wave 1 (evidence-layer partnerships with reporting suites and sustainability consultancies) and the €490 pilot programme that trades a nominal price for publishable case studies. In parallel, product depth: an ESRS E1 datapoint-coverage map in every report, E1-9 anticipated-financial-effects context from documented loss sources, and scenario / time-horizon fields in evidence packs.

Stage 3 · Future

Suite integrations, trust enablers and data-as-a-service

Integration formats and a partner API so GRC/ESG suites can consume Talaix as their physical-risk evidence layer; a security/GDPR trust page for enterprise procurement (ISO 27001 / SOC 2 gap list); procurement of commercial catastrophe-loss database licences (e.g. Munich Re NatCatSERVICE, Swiss Re sigma) after first platform revenue; and a climate-intelligence API / data-as-a-service offering.

Heritage

From wildfire R&D to compliance evidence

Talaix's analysis pipeline began with wildfire, grew into a multi-hazard evidence engine, and since 2026-09-06 fronts compliance: the engine is unchanged — what changed is who pays and why. Mandatory EU disclosure deadlines, not discretionary analytics, now lead the product.
