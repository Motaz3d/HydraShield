Product Roadmap

From evidence engine to financial-intelligence products — a staged roadmap that prioritises honest, documented data over speculative claims.

Stages

Building the evidence layer for financial decisions

Honest, non-committal timing: capabilities ship when the underlying data and validation are ready; no capability is released with invented figures.

Stage 1 · Now

Multi-hazard evidence engine + report products

A single evidence base for seven hazards — wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind, coastal exposure and tropical cyclones — powering report products for green-finance verification, insurance risk, CSRD/ESRS sustainability evidence, supply-chain origin screening and environmental forensics.

Stage 2 · In progress

Documented loss figures, licensing advisory and investment siting

Integration of documented loss figures from free open sources; Environmental Licensing advisory dossiers backed by satellite evidence; investment-siting intelligence that matches multi-hazard exposure to sector siting needs and public funding programmes.

Stage 3 · Future

Commercial loss databases, time-horizon analysis and data-as-a-service

Procurement of commercial catastrophe-loss database licences (e.g. Munich Re NatCatSERVICE, Swiss Re sigma) is planned after first platform revenue; operator decision target 2026-09-02. Also planned: time-horizon investment analysis tools and a climate-intelligence API / data-as-a-service offering.

Heritage

From wildfire R&D to multi-hazard finance intelligence

Talaix's analysis pipeline began with wildfire. That heritage remains one of seven hazards today; the company is repositioning around the broader financial and investment use cases the same evidence base can serve.
