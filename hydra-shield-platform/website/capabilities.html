Financial risk from climate, for insurance first

Whichever door you use, the output is the same kind of thing: documented physical climate-risk evidence, with the source, date, confidence and declared gaps attached to every figure. The doors are ordered for how most users arrive — insurance first, financing second, EU compliance as a specialist tool.
