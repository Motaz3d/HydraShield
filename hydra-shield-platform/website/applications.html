Deployment Scenarios

Real-World Applications

Phase 1 will simulate interventions across distinct topologies in European WUI testbeds.

🏘️ Small Mediterranean Village

Scenario: High-consequence WUI zone with single-road access and limited water resources.

  • Concentrated WUI with single-access evacuation route
  • Limited water availability
  • High population density in fire-prone area
  • Priority: Secure evacuation corridor

HydraShield Strategy: Optimize subsurface hydration along the evacuation corridor to delay fire progression and secure safe egress.

⛰️ Mountainous WUI

Scenario: High-slope terrain focusing on downslope and flanking fire progression under wind-driven scenarios.

  • Steep terrain with complex fire behavior
  • Wind-driven fire spread
  • Limited access for firefighting equipment
  • Priority: Protect residential clusters

HydraShield Strategy: Model downslope and flanking fire progression to identify critical protection zones on slopes.

🏜️ Sandy Soil Region

Scenario: Testing rapid drainage and limited capillary rise in sandy soil environments.

  • Rapid water drainage
  • Limited capillary rise
  • Low water retention capacity
  • Priority: Evaluate hybrid strategies

HydraShield Strategy: Evaluate hybrid surface wetting fallback for sandy soils where subsurface hydration may be less effective.

🏙️ Urban Fringe

Scenario: Protecting city outskirts containing highly flammable green spaces.

  • Urban-adjacent green spaces
  • High-value infrastructure nearby
  • Dense population at risk
  • Priority: Protect critical infrastructure

HydraShield Strategy: Create protective moisture corridors around urban fringes to prevent fire ingress into residential areas.
