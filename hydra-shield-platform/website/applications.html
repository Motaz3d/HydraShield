Who Uses Talaix

The same evidence base — seven hazards, real data, honest labels — answers different questions for different decisions. Every answer below is the same product: analysis, exposure, economy, solutions, monitoring and alerts.

Sectors

One Evidence Base, Many Decisions

🏦 Insurance & finance

Underwriting context, portfolio screening and investment due diligence need traceable exposure evidence — not black-box scores.

  • Per-location hazard screening with provenance
  • Population, asset and critical-facility exposure
  • Historical event evidence per area

🏙️ Municipalities & government

Civil protection and resilience planning need current conditions, historical events and monitored places — with alerts that reach people outside the website.

  • Multi-hazard maps for the territory
  • Monitoring + SMS/email alerts on meaningful change
  • Evidence-labelled reports for plans and grants

⚡ Energy, transport & ports

Infrastructure operators need to know which assets sit in hazard-exposed areas — and when conditions at those assets change.

  • Asset-location screening across seven hazards
  • Coastal, wind, heat and flood context per site
  • Monitoring rules per asset location

🌾 Agriculture & land

Drought, heat and wildfire shape seasons. Cropland exposure and historical conditions support planning decisions.

  • Drought and heat screening vs climatology
  • Cropland exposure from ESA WorldCover
  • Historical conditions per season

🏗️ Real estate & construction

Site selection and disclosure increasingly require documented climate exposure — current and historical, with sources.

  • Site screening with evidence labels
  • Coastal and flood exposure for assets
  • Reports with traceable methodology

🔬 Research & universities

Reproducible, provenance-carrying analysis runs and open registries support method comparison and teaching.

  • Content-hashed analysis runs
  • Public source, model and ground-truth registries
  • API + SDKs for scripted research
