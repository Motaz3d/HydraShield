What is inside every pack

Site and method

The coordinates analysed, the run date, how many hazards were screened and the evidence-label vocabulary used throughout.

Per-hazard evidence

One row per hazard with its level — and, in a customer pack, the dataset, its version, the date range covered and the confidence behind it.

Compliance mapping

Which disclosure areas the evidence covers — for example ESRS E1 physical risk covered_by_evidence, other areas declared not_covered rather than implied.

Declared gaps and disclaimer

Where data could not be read, the pack says UNKNOWN and lists it. Plus the honesty contract, the scope limits and the “not assurance” disclaimer.

Run your own coordinates

A place name, a lat/lon pair, or a list of sites. The first check is free; a decision-ready pack is €19 and a scientific report €39.
