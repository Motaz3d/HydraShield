## Programme detail

### 1. EIT Jumpstarter

- **Name:** EIT Jumpstarter
- **Operator / funder:** EIT — European Institute of Innovation and Technology (an EU body), delivered by the EIT Knowledge and Innovation Communities ("powered by 7 EIT KICs": EIT Health, EIT Food, EIT RawMaterials / InnoEnergy, EIT Urban Mobility, EIT Culture & Creativity, EIT Water, plus a Maltese cohort).
- **Country / coverage:** **not all-EU, not global.** At least one team member must hold citizenship of an eligible country/territory: Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Spain; the EU Outermost Regions (Guadeloupe, French Guiana, Réunion, Martinique, Mayotte, Saint-Martin; Azores, Madeira; Canary Islands); and the Horizon Europe Associated Countries Albania, Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Ukraine, Türkiye. **Finland and Luxembourg are not on the list** — the programme is aimed at Central, Eastern and Southern Europe.
- **Focus:** pre-acceleration for "idea/solution owners" who want to start a company, within the EIT KIC thematic areas: Food & Agritech; Healthcare; Energy & Renewables; Smart Cities & Urban Mobility; Metals, Minerals and Industrial Side Streams (Raw Materials); plus a Maltese start-up cohort. The "Advanced and Green Manufacturing" category is **cancelled for the 2026 edition** (submissions redistributed among other categories).
- **Does it accept a pre-incorporation team?** **Yes — the programme is built for teams with no company yet, and actively requires it.** Official Terms & Conditions v2 (06.05.2026), §2.1, verbatim: *"Only applicants without a registered startup (based on the submitted idea) before 1 January 2026 are eligible. Those with a legal entity registered before that date, tied to the submitted idea, are ineligible. Companies registered from 1 January 2026 are eligible."* §2.1 also states *"Individuals or teams may apply"* (max. two co-founding members eligible for training). Eligibility additionally requires the applicant to be ready to set up a new enterprise by end of 2026; at the Business Plan stage (§1.2.6) the team submits *"a Registration certificate or Proof of Filing"* or, in justified cases, *"a Letter of Intent to incorporate by end of December 2026"*.
- **Format:** hybrid — 15 hours of online bootcamp (business basics, pitching practice) plus 40 hours of in-person training (business plan development, soft skills); the Grand Finale / Grand Demo Day is in person (2024 and 2025 editions in Budapest). **Relocation:** not stated on the official page — eligibility is citizenship-based and there is no residency condition; a travel budget is provided.
- **Duration:** 7 months (the site describes it as "a 7-month pre-acceleration programme").
- **Funding / equity / terms:** up to **€10,000** first prize; prize tiers €2,500 / €5,000 / €10,000, plus a travel budget (€2,000 + €1,000). The 2026 edition was announced with a **€240,000** prize pot. **No equity is mentioned anywhere on the official pages** (not stated that equity is taken). Extra rule: the sum of EIT grants already received by the applicant must not exceed **€60,000**. Special prizes of €2,500 each for best Western Balkans team, best EU Outermost Regions team, best Ukrainian team, best New European Bauhaus-aligned team.
- **Application window / next deadline:** **CLOSED.** The 2026 call deadline was **17 May 2026**; the site currently states applications are closed and that cohort selection results will be announced in June, directing visitors to follow social media for the next edition. **The next edition is not announced.**
- **Official URL:** https://eitjumpstarter.eu/ · Terms & Conditions: https://eitjumpstarter.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/EIT-Jumpstarter-2026_Terms-and-Conditions_v2_20260506.pdf (both HTTP 200 on 2026-09-21)
- **Fit for Talaix:** the *pre-incorporation rule fits perfectly* (a company registered during 2026 — i.e. the planned Q4 2026 Luxembourg S.à.r.l. — is exactly what the programme wants), but **Talaix is blocked on eligibility**: neither Luxembourg nor Finland appears on the eligible-country list and the rule is citizenship-based ("at least one team member must hold citizenship in an eligible country"). Even setting that aside, the 2026 round is closed and no next round is announced. **Verdict: not applicable.**
