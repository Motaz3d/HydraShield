# Pre-incubation programmes across Europe — index and action list

**Date:** 21 September 2026
**Purpose:** one entry point for the pre-incubation / pre-acceleration landscape, with an honest statement of what is verified and what is not.

## How this was built, and why it is incomplete

The sweep was split into three detail files (see the map below). Two of the three were produced by automated research agents that **hit their turn limit before finishing**: one wrote a skeleton with no facts, and two produced exactly one well-sourced programme each. The remaining entries were then verified by hand, and several programme sites (fit4start.lu, esabic.lu, esa-bic-bavaria.de) either serve no readable content or do not resolve, so their terms could not be read.

**The result is a verified core plus a large explicitly-unverified backlog.** Nothing in these files is inferred from third-party lists, and no deadline, amount or eligibility rule has been guessed. Where a field could not be read from the programme's own page, it says so. Treat this as a first pass to be extended, not as a complete market map.

## Method rule for whoever continues this

Every claim must come from the programme's own official page, read live. Write "not stated on the official page" for anything absent — never infer a deadline, amount or eligibility rule. Mark each entry "verified live <date>" or "unverified". A programme that requires an existing legal entity is a **finding**, not a failure, and should be recorded as such.
