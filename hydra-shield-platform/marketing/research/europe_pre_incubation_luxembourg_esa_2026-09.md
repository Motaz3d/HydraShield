## Detail

### 1. Fit 4 Start — Luxembourg

- **Operator:** initiated by the **Ministry of the Economy**, managed by **Luxinnovation**.
- **What the official page says (verbatim, LSA funding page, last update 11.06.2026):** "a leading acceleration programme for innovative early-stage start-ups **looking to incorporate in Luxembourg** and build a strong local presence"; "one edition per year; spans up to two years, including a **6-month intensive coaching phase**"; benefits described as accelerating business growth, immersion in the Luxembourg start-up ecosystem, and connecting to a network of experts, partners and investors.
- **Source:** https://space-agency.public.lu/en/support/industry-portal/funding.html (fetched 2026-09-21).
- **Pre-incorporation:** the phrase "looking to incorporate in Luxembourg" indicates the programme is aimed at companies not yet established there, but **this is a third-party description, not Fit 4 Start's own eligibility rule. UNVERIFIED.**
- **UNVERIFIED and needed:** the official eligibility wording (does a team with no legal entity qualify?), the funding amount, whether equity or a convertible is taken, the current cohort status and the next application deadline.
- **Why it matters for Talaix:** this is the local programme — the founder lives in Luxembourg and plans a Luxembourg S.à.r.l. in Q4 2026, which is exactly the "incorporate in Luxembourg" profile the programme describes. It should be the first call, and the gap in this file is the reason to make it.

### 2. ESA BIC Luxembourg

- **Operator chain (verbatim, LSA homepage, fetched 2026-09-21):** ESA BIC Luxembourg is "initiated by the Ministry of the Economy and managed by Luxinnovation, in collaboration with Technoport, the Luxembourg Space Agency (LSA)".
- **Leadership change noted on the same page (2026-09-21):** Marc Serres has departed the LSA and **Ruy Pinto was appointed interim CEO on 15.09.2026**. This is directly relevant to the open LSA thread — see the note at the end of this file.
- **UNVERIFIED:** eligibility for a pre-incorporation team, the funding amount, the incubation duration, and the current open-call window. `esabic.lu` does not resolve (HTTP 000) and the Luxinnovation programme page returned HTTP 404, so the programme's own terms could not be read.
- **Source:** https://space-agency.public.lu/en.html

### 3. ESA BIC Poland

- **Operator:** **Agencja Rozwoju Przemysłu S.A. (ARP S.A.)**, Nowy Świat 6/12, 00-400 Warszawa; contact office@esabic.pl.
- **Offer (verbatim from the site):** funding — **incentive up to €50,000**; **Boost up to €100,000 for 2 start-ups per year**; technical support up to **80 h** of expert advice; business development/mentoring up to **50 h**; administrative support (workspace); legal consulting up to **10 h**; use of the ESA BIC brand; access to the international ESA BIC network; international promotion.
- **Eligibility conditions stated on the open-call update:** the company must have **Polish nationality**, verified via the esa-star self-assessment form **at contract signing**; and a **strict non-military policy** — neither incubation nor incentive funding may be used for military activities (peaceful activities only). There is also a stated policy on reapplying.
- **Application window — AMBIGUITY, DO NOT ASSUME:** the ESA BIC Poland home page states an **application deadline of 23 October 2026**. A separate page, "Open Call documentation update", refers to submissions for "the deadline of **October 10th**" and states that all submissions must use the new document templates. The update page appears to relate to an earlier round. **Confirm the correct 2026 deadline with office@esabic.pl before relying on it.**
- **Sources:** https://www.esabic.pl/ · https://esabic.pl/open-call-documentation-update/ (both fetched 2026-09-21)
- **Fit for Talaix:** the funding structure and the pre-incorporation-friendly interview process already in the pipeline (see PLAN.md) make this the most concrete ESA BIC route — but the **Polish nationality requirement at contract signing** is the decision point, and the operator's Finnish/EU citizenship does not satisfy it. Resolve that with ARP before spending more effort.

### 5. LHoFT — Luxembourg
- Fintech innovation hub/foundation, founded 2016. Runs incubation, acceleration and fellowship programmes, co-hosted events, corporate innovation and education; provides coworking space. Stated figures: 211 supported fintechs, 190 accelerated start-ups, 29 countries represented, 300+ mentors and experts.
- Two newsroom items worth noting for company formation: **Luxembourg's new employee-stock-option tax framework (21 July 2026)** and the inauguration of an **AI Experience Centre at the LHoFT**.
- **Fit for Talaix:** limited — the focus is fintech, whereas Talaix is physical-risk evidence sold into financial *and* corporate buyers. The ESOP tax framework is the useful takeaway, not the programme.
- **Source:** https://www.lhoft.com/ (fetched 2026-09-21)

### 6. Technoport — Luxembourg
- Positions itself as "Powering Europe's Deep Tech Future"; deep-tech focus with named areas **Space Tech, Health Tech, Defense Tech, Automobility**; offers resources, mentorship and a collaborative environment. Technoport is also a named collaborator in ESA BIC Luxembourg.
- **Fit for Talaix:** relevant as the deep-tech host and as the ESA BIC LU collaborator; no programme terms are published on the pages read.
- **Source:** https://www.technoport.lu/ (fetched 2026-09-21)
