# Europe — national climate / Earth-observation / AI pre-incubation programmes

**Research date:** 21 September 2026
**Scope:** national (not EU-institution, not ESA BIC) pre-incubation / accelerator
programmes relevant to a **pre-incorporation** climate-risk Earth-observation + AI
startup (Talaix — CSRD/ESRS E1, EU Taxonomy DNSH, EUDR evidence packs).
**Limit:** maximum 6 programmes; depth over breadth.

**Method:** each programme was opened live with `web_fetch` on the date shown in its
section, on the programme's own official domain. Nothing is inferred from
third-party lists. Fields absent from the official page are written as
"not stated on the official page" — never guessed. A programme that requires an
existing legal entity is reported as such (a useful finding, not a failure).

**Countries NOT covered in this pass:** Belgium, France, Poland, Czechia, Hungary,
Romania, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Slovenia, Lithuania, Latvia, Sweden, Iceland, Malta,
Cyprus, Turkey, and all non-European jurisdictions. Also excluded by scope: the
ESA BIC network (Noordwijk, Poland, Luxembourg…) and EU-institution programmes
(EIC, LIFE, Horizon).

## Detail

### 1. greenstart — Klima- und Energiefonds (Austria)

- **Operator:** Klima- und Energiefonds (Austrian federal climate & energy fund). "Greenstart ist der Start-up Accelerator des Klima- und Energiefonds."
- **City / country:** Vienna, Austria (Leopold-Ungar-Platz 2, 1190 Wien).
- **Focus:** green business models — climate-change mitigation and **adaptation**, energy efficiency, renewables, circular economy, agriculture/bioeconomy. Recent TOP 10 included a climate-adaptation multi-sensor network (Monitorius) and AI soil analysis (AGAi).
- **Pre-incorporation accepted: YES.** Eligibility wording verbatim: *"Privatpersonen mit innovativen Ideen und dem Ziel der Ausgründung"* (private individuals with innovative ideas and the goal of founding a company) — listed as the first target group, alongside start-ups in the founding phase, start-ups up to 3 years old, and associations/cooperatives/small companies developing a new field of business. No legal entity is required for the first stage.
- **Format:** two-stage competition; a 6-month acceleration phase with targeted workshops, coaching (10 h personal coach + 10 h expert consultation), network access and public-relations support. Delivery is in-person/event-based in Austria (workshops, KEM specialist event, Release Gala); remote operation is **not stated on the official page**.
- **Relocation:** **not stated on the official page** as a requirement, but the goal is explicitly "das Potenzial innovativer und grüner Geschäftsmodelle **in Österreich** zu erschließen" (to unlock green business-model potential *in Austria*) — the programme is de-facto Austria-oriented.
- **Duration:** 6-month acceleration phase.
- **Funding / terms:** €10,000 per start-up for the TOP 10 (stage 1); €20,000 per start-up for the TOP 3 (stage 2). Equity: **none mentioned** — it is prize money from a public fund, not an investment.
- **Application window / next deadline:** **CLOSED.** FAQ: *"Aktuell ist keine Bewerbung möglich."* Latest completed edition is greenstart 10 (2025); the TOP 3 were announced at the Release Event on 27 Nov 2025. On the next call: *"Wann startet die nächste Ausschreibung? Abonniere den Newsletter um von Neuigkeiten als Erste:r zu erfahren"* — i.e. **next round unannounced**, newsletter sign-up only. Submission is via the Klima- und Energiefonds website; required documents are the official application form plus the applicant's CV with references.
- **Official URLs:** https://www.greenstart.at/ · https://greenstart.at/einreichen/faq/ · funder: https://www.klimafonds.gv.at/
- **Verified:** verified live 2026-09-21 (both pages HTTP 200, read in full).

**Fit for Talaix:** Strong thematic fit — climate adaptation is a named category and an AI/monitoring start-up (Monitorius) reached the TOP 10 in 2025. The "individuals aiming to found" target group is exactly Talaix's pre-incorporation state, and €10k–20k with no equity is non-dilutive. Two frictions: (a) the call is **closed with no announced date**, so it cannot be planned into the 24-month roadmap; (b) it is de-facto an Austria-domestic programme, while Talaix's market is EU-wide from Luxembourg. Worth a newsletter subscription and a calendar re-check, not a strategic dependency.
