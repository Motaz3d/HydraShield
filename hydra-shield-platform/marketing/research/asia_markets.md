# Asia market intelligence (research framework)

**Status:** framework only — no researched organizations yet. This note
defines the structure; records enter the workspace only from official
sources with `date_checked`.

## Priority geographies

China · Japan · South Korea · Singapore · India · ASEAN (Indonesia,
Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia)

## Why these (hazard-driven, documented)

- Heat, flood, typhoon (wind), coastal and drought exposure are documented
  across the region by WMO/national agencies — the commercial entry point
  is the hazard, not the market size (no market-size claims are made).
- Dense infrastructure, ports, energy and agriculture create the exposure
  contexts Talaix analyzes.

## The connection model (per country)

```
hazards (documented) → regions → exposed sectors → organizations →
projects → investment/funding → procurement → Talaix relevance
```

## Rules

- Research only official sources: national agencies, official development
  banks (ADB, AIIB), official project/procurement portals, institutional
  pages.
- No invented organizations, contacts, projects or budgets.
- Records go into `marketing/leads/`, `marketing/signals/`,
  `marketing/procurement/` with the standard provenance fields.
- Language/localization of outreach is a later, deliberate step.
