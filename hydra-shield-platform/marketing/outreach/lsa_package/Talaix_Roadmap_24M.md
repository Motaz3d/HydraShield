## 5. How the customer targets are built

- Prices are published, not aspirational: €19 per report, €49–249/month subscriptions, €490 pilots, Government from €4,900/year, Enterprise from €12,000/year.
- The demand driver is a legal deadline: CSRD wave-2 companies must report on financial year 2027 (reports published 2028), so 2027 is the buying season the Q4 2027 push is timed for. EUDR applies since 30 December 2025.
- Outreach is already running: waves to CSRD-reporting companies, insurers and sustainability consultants are scheduled and sending this month; the pilot programme converts early customers into publishable case studies, which unlock the slow segments (insurance, banking, government).
- The mix behind the targets: mostly self-serve Professional/Business subscriptions, a small number of Government and Enterprise contracts from Q4 2027.

## 6. Honest note

The platform is pre-revenue today — deliberately: the funnel, pricing and outreach were finished first. These targets are planning figures, not promises; they exist so that time, money and customers can be tracked against them quarterly, and corrected in the open.
