## الفقرة الجوهر لكل الرسائل (Core Paragraph)

Talaix is a Luxembourg-based technology initiative building evidence-labelled climate and environmental extreme-risk intelligence. Open source is at its core - a Python library, with a QGIS plugin in the pipeline. We compute seven hazards - wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind, coastal exposure and tropical cyclones. We combine Earth observation, official open data and documented historical events, and we count what and who is exposed - population, settlements, hospitals and schools, buildings, transport, energy, water, tourism, industry. Every result carries its source, method, resolutionand evidence status. Observed data is kept separate from projections. Unavailable data is stated, and no invented figures is an operating rule, not a slogan. Everything is labelled as a screening indicator, never as a validated prediction. Multi-hazard exposure supports adaptation, civil protection, insurance and disclosure workflows - EU Taxonomy, CSRD, TCFD/ISSB.

## خطوات التشغيل

1) اختبار ذاتي أولاً - هذه المعاينة تجرى اليوم.
   cd /opt/hydrashield && .venv/bin/python scripts/send_preview_funder_wave.py
   ستصلك رسالة على info@talaix.com تعرض صياغة نموذجية لمثال EIC, وتُسجل في data/outbox إذا لم يُضبط SMTP.د

3) الإرسال الفعلي - يدوياً من Vultr عبر سكربت موجة يُبنى بعد موافقتك, مع التزام: سقف يومي 20 رسالة, وإلغاء الاشتراك عبر الرد unsubscribe, وبترتيب Tier 1 أولاً ثم Tier 2 ثم Tier 3 مع مرور أسبوع بين كل موجة.ث


4) التوثيق - كل رسالة تُسجل interaction على lead, وتُرقى outreach_status إلى contacted, والردود تُوثق في السجلات.
