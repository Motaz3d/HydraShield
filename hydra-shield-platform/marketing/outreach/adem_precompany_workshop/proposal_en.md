## 1. Summary

Unemployment among highly qualified people over 40 is one of the highest and still rising. Standard reintegration offers rarely fit this group: they do not need basic training — they need a structured way to convert deep professional experience into economic activity of their own.

This proposal is a **3-month intensive workshop** in which 8–12 participants each build and real-world-test a **pre-company project** — a validated initiative one step before founding a company — using **artificial intelligence and automation** as their operating infrastructure. The trainer shares the participants' profile (Master's-level, over 40, currently jobseeking) and works inside the cohort as a participant himself: a peer-led format, offered for the first time at ADEM.

## 2. The problem

- Jobseekers over 40 with higher education face long unemployment durations and a rising risk of permanent exclusion, despite — or because of — their qualifications.
- The classic labour market undervalues their experience; yet that experience is exactly the asset from which independent activity can be built.
- Founding a company directly is risky and expensive. What is missing is a structured, low-cost way to **test a project in the real market before incorporation** — the pre-company stage.

## 3. The concept: pre-company projects built with AI

- Each participant's professional specialty — whatever it is — is mapped and converted into a concrete initiative. **Every case is studied individually**; solutions are designed per specialization, however diverse the backgrounds.
- AI is used as working infrastructure, not as a topic of theory:
  - **AI in marketing** — positioning, content, campaign drafts, audience testing.
  - **AI as a secretary** — automation of scheduled routine tasks: correspondence, sending, follow-ups, appointments.
  - **Visibility and media** — preparing press-ready material, contacting media outlets, building a public professional identity.
- **Real-world testing**: projects are exposed to real customers and partners during the course. By the end, each participant holds evidence — not just a plan.

## 4. Why this solves a real bottleneck

- With AI, one person can now carry out research, marketing, administration and outreach that used to require a small team — the cost of experimentation collapses.
- Testing before founding reduces risk for the participant and for public funds alike.
- The format builds **pre-company skills**: opportunity recognition, validation, customer contact, digital identity — valuable even if a participant later chooses employment over founding.

## 6. Programme structure — 3 months, intensive (12 weeks)

**Phase 1 (weeks 1–3) — Skills audit and opportunity mapping**
Inventory of each participant's experience; AI-assisted analysis of where it meets real market demand. Building the professional identity: *what is your specialty — everyone can build their identity.*

**Phase 2 (weeks 4–6) — Designing the initiative and the AI toolbox**
Case-by-case sessions: each specialty gets its own solution path. Setting up the personal AI toolkit: research, writing, planning; AI as a secretary — automating scheduled routine tasks and sending.

**Phase 3 (weeks 7–9) — Marketing and visibility**
AI-assisted marketing; building public presence; preparing media material; contacting media outlets.

**Phase 4 (weeks 10–12) — Real-world testing and validation**
Pilot offers to real customers and partners; collecting evidence; go/pivot decision; individual six-month roadmap (founding, freelancing, or employment); final presentation.

**Format:** 2 sessions per week (3 hours each) plus individual coaching slots; in person in Luxembourg with online tooling; group of 8–12 participants; instruction in English with support in French, German and Arabic.

## 7. Expected outcomes

Per participant:
- a concrete pre-company project, tested in the real market, with documented results;
- a personal AI workflow (marketing, administrative automation, outreach);
- a public professional identity with media-ready material;
- a grounded decision — found, freelance, or employ — with a six-month roadmap.

For ADEM: measurable activation of a hard-to-place group; projects that can later be taken up by existing ADEM support measures.
