## 1. Your 3-minute opening (say it, then offer the live demo)

Talaix is a multi-hazard climate-risk intelligence platform, built in Luxembourg. It aggregates Earth-observation data — Copernicus Sentinel-2, Landsat, FIRMS, ERA5 and around 25 integrated open datasets — to analyse ten natural hazards and produce evidence-linked risk reports for banks, insurers, governments and investors. The platform is live today, with user accounts, an API, a reports portal, and an Academy for climate-risk literacy. Our market focus since this month is climate-risk compliance evidence for EU disclosure rules — CSRD and the EU Taxonomy. We are pre-company and pre-seed: this call is about which Luxembourg instruments fit to turn a working platform into a Luxembourg space-data company.

Then offer: "I can show you the live platform in five minutes — the hazard map, a real analysis report, and the compliance evidence page."

## 2. The asks — restructured (say them in this order)

**A. Incubation & company formation (your main ask)**
"I want to establish the company in Luxembourg. Which track fits a pre-company DeepTech project like this — ESA BIC Luxembourg, LuxIMPULSE, or another national programme? Can one apply before incorporation, and what are the criteria and the next deadlines?"
(ESA BIC = funding + technical support + credibility; this is also the legitimate route to later co-branding — see D.)

**B. Data access & reliability**
"The platform already runs on open Copernicus and ESA-family data. To reach a higher reliability tier, I would like access to higher-resolution or commercial data sources — ESA Third Party Missions, contributing missions, or voucher schemes — and ideally a validation partner. What mechanisms exist for that?"
Honest line to keep: "Our assessments are screening-level today; a documented validation roadmap is exactly where agency support would raise us."

**C. Alerts (SMS) — the reframed version. NEVER say "competes with government services".**
"We built a multi-channel alerting component for environmental hazards — email and SMS — which today runs in a development outbox until an operator is configured. We see it as complementary to national alerting like LU-Alert, not competing with it: a specialised layer for climate-risk events tied to assets and locations users care about. Is there a pilot framework with civil protection or a public body where this could be tested properly?"

**D. Credibility / logo — do NOT ask to put the LSA logo on the site.**
Ask instead: "Which partnerships carry co-branding rights — for example, do ESA BIC start-ups get to use programme branding? We want credibility the correct way, through an actual programme." (Asking for a logo in call one signals the wrong priorities.)

**E. Research & academic partnership**
"We are looking for research partners — LIST's remote-sensing work and the University of Luxembourg (SnT) are natural fits. The Academy, our climate-risk literacy track, is built on open material and we would welcome review or co-development — does LSA run or connect to education initiatives such as ESERO Luxembourg? I can send the Academy modules for review after the call."

**F. Funding partnership**
"Beyond incubation: which funding instruments should we line up next — ESA Business Applications feasibility studies, Horizon Europe via the national contact points, EU space-entrepreneurship programmes like Cassini? We are preparing a pre-seed round of €850K; programme co-funding would de-risk it."

**G. Dual-use / rescue (one sentence only, offer a written follow-up)**
"Our analytics also serve emergency response and dual-use cases — for example EO-based situational awareness for aerial firefighting and rescue operations, where Luxembourg has airborne assets. If that interests the Agency, I will send a short written note after the call rather than take time now."

**H. Network**
"And honestly, I am looking for the network: which three organisations or people should I speak to next in the Luxembourg ecosystem?"

## 5. Red lines — do not say

- "Can we put your logo on our site" — ask about programme branding rights instead.
- Any claim of validated accuracy — the platform is screening-level; honesty is the credibility.
- The rescue-helicopter story as a complaint — frame positively as "aerial response analytics", one sentence max.
- Don't mention the ADEM workshop or unemployment — this call is about the company, not personal situation.
