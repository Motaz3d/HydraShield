## 1. Climate-KIC (المسار داخل المنظمة، وليس افتراض ClimateLaunchpad)

- البريد: **ckbrussels@climate-kic.org** (بنلوكس/بروكسل — يغطي لوكسمبورغ) — من صفحة الاتصال الرسمية climate-kic.org، فحص 2026-08-31.
- البديل (ألمانيا): germany@climate-kic.org
- الموضوع: Talaix - Earth-observation and climate-risk intelligence; which Climate-KIC path fits best
- النص التنفيذي:

Talaix is building an Earth-observation and climate-risk intelligence layer
that translates environmental and satellite data into decision intelligence
for finance, insurance, government and real estate. It is open source at the
core — a Python library with a QGIS plugin in the pipeline — computing seven
hazards and counting exposed people and assets, every result traceable to its
source and labelled as a screening indicator.

Rather than assume a specific programme, we would like your guidance on which
path inside Climate-KIC best fits a Luxembourg-based, pre-company initiative
of this kind — ClimateLaunchpad, ClimAccelerator, a partnership, or something
else — and who the right regional contact is.

- الطلب: تحديد المسار المناسب داخل Climate-KIC + جهة الاتصال الإقليمية الصحيحة.

## 2. EUSPA (use case: EO → physical risk → financial decision intelligence)

- البريد: **market@euspa.europa.eu** (فريق السوق/الابتكار) — مُدرج أيضاً في الموجة 1 بفحص 2026-08-31.
- الموضوع: Talaix - a downstream EO use case: physical risk to financial decision intelligence
- النص التنفيذي:

Talaix turns Earth-observation data into physical climate-risk indicators that
feed financial decision intelligence — for insurance, banking, government and
real estate. It is an open-source Python engine with a QGIS plugin, computing
seven hazards from Copernicus and open data, with exposure counts and full
source provenance.

We would like to know which CASSINI challenge or EUSPA funding/prize
opportunity best fits a downstream EO application of this kind, and whom to
contact for the next open call.

- الطلب: تحديد أقرب فرصة CASSINI/EUSPA + جهة الاتصال للدورة القادمة.
