## الفقرة الجوهر (Core Paragraph)

Talaix is a climate-risk compliance-evidence platform. From Earth
observation, official open data and documented historical events it produces
physical climate-risk evidence for EU disclosure — CSRD/ESRS E1 hazard
screening at site coordinates, EU Taxonomy DNSH adaptation vocabulary, and
EUDR geolocation screening — with every result carrying its source, date,
method and evidence status (OBSERVED through UNKNOWN). The analytical engine
is open source (EUPL-1.2), and machine-readable XBRL output is generated by
the CsrdTX rules-as-data engine. Talaix is an evidence layer, not assurance:
limited assurance stays with the auditor. Prices are published — a free
tier, pay-per-report, and subscriptions from €49/month — no enterprise sales
cycle.
