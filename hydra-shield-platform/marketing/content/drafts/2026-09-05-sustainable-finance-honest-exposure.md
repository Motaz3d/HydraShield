In sustainable finance, the most honest sentence is often: "this is not
quantified — and here is why."

Climate exposure evidence loses value the moment it pretends to be a
price. So Talaix separates two things that are routinely blurred:

- Exposure evidence — which hazards affect this location, from which
  sources, with which methods, periods and confidence. Always stated.
- Economic meaning — stated where a real valuation basis exists, and
  explicitly marked "not quantified" where it does not.

For DNSH screening and green-finance decisions this matters practically:
a credit or allocation decision can point to traceable per-asset evidence
instead of a black-box score — and the disclosure file shows the
limitation instead of hiding it.

A number without a source is a liability, not a feature.
