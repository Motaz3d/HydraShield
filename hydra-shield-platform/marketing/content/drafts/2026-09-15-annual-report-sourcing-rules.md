An annual climate-extremes report is only worth publishing if every
sentence in it can survive the question: "says who, measured how?"

The sourcing rules for the Talaix Annual Climate Extreme Intelligence
Report are public before the report itself:

- Events enter only from authoritative archives and instrument feeds —
  NASA FIRMS detections per sensor, official discharge gauges, ERA5
  climatology against a named baseline.
- Every claim carries its temporal class — OBSERVED, HISTORICAL,
  FORECAST, PROJECTED — and its evidence record: source, method,
  period, resolution, confidence, license, limitations.
- Cause is DOCUMENTED or UNKNOWN. It is never inferred from media
  coverage or from a model.
- What the data cannot support is stated as unavailable — the gap is
  published, not worked around.

This is slower than writing a report from headlines. It is also the only
version we are willing to sign.
