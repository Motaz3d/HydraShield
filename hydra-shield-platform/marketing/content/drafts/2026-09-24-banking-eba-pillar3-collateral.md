EBA's ESG risk guidelines and Pillar 3 disclosures share one practical
consequence for banks: climate risk is now measured at the level of
individual exposures, not portfolio narratives.

That means the collateral file and the loan book need per-location
answers:

- What is the physical hazard profile of this property, this plant,
  this logistics site — across six hazards, from named sources?
- Which parts of that profile are observed history, and which are
  modelled projections with a stated baseline?
- Can every figure in the disclosure schedule be traced back to a
  dataset, a period and a method?

Traceability is the point supervisors keep returning to. An exposure
table whose cells each carry provenance survives scrutiny; a table of
unlabelled scores invites questions no one wants in a supervisory
dialogue.
