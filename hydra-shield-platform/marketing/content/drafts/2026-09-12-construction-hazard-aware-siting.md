The cheapest climate adaptation in construction is the decision made
before concrete.

A site screen answers six questions with evidence, not brochures:
wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind, coastal exposure.
Each answer is labelled — OBSERVED, HISTORICAL, MODELLED or PROJECTED —
with the source dataset and its period named on the page.

Two things this changes in practice:

- Siting: exposure evidence belongs in the site-selection file, next to
  geotechnics — not discovered at insurance renewal.
- Design justification: when an adaptation measure is specified, the
  hazard evidence behind it is documented, and its limitations are
  stated. No vendor promises, no invented return periods.
