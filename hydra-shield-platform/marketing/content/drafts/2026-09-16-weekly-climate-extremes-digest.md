Every week, somewhere, the data changes before the news cycle notices.

This is what a live multi-hazard watch actually looks at — not headlines,
but instrument feeds:

- Heat: today's anomaly against the 1991–2020 ERA5 climatology, as a
  percentile — so "hot" has a named baseline, not a vibe
- Wildfire: NASA FIRMS active-fire detections, per sensor, never merged
  into an unlabelled blend
- Flood: modelled discharge against climatology, labelled as modelled —
  with gauge observations shown separately where they exist
- Drought: precipitation anomaly and soil-moisture balance over the
  period, not a single dramatic day

For a municipality, the point is not the map — it is the moment a
threshold you chose is crossed, and an alert reaches the right person by
email or SMS. Meaningful change only; no alarm fatigue.
