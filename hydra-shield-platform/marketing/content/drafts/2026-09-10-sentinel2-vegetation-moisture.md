What did the satellite actually see?

In wildfire and drought work, one of the most useful Sentinel-2 products
is vegetation moisture — not as a "fire risk score", but as what it is:
an observed spectral signal, from a named sensor, on a named date, at a
named resolution.

That distinction is the whole point. When Talaix shows vegetation
moisture as evidence, the layer carries its provenance with it — sensor,
acquisition, method. It is never merged with modelled context into an
unlabelled blend, and where coverage is missing the gap is shown, not
interpolated away.

For GIS people: this arrives as layers with per-layer provenance —
GeoJSON grids, an analysis API, and a QGIS plugin in early access — not
as screenshots of a dashboard.
