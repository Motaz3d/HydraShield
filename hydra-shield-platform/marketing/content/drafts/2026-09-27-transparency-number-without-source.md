Transparency series #2: A number without a source is a liability, not a
feature.

Climate risk outputs tend to arrive as single scores: 7.2/10, "high",
red. They read as knowledge. They are usually the opposite — the method,
the baseline, the dataset and the uncertainty all compressed into a
digit that cannot be interrogated.

The test we apply to every number Talaix publishes: can a skeptical
professional — a validator, a supervisor, a reviewer — trace it back?

That means each figure carries its evidence record: source, dataset,
period, method, resolution, confidence, license, limitations. And where
a valuation basis does not exist, the output says "not quantified"
rather than manufacturing a decimal.

This costs us the marketing appeal of a simple score. What it buys is
the only thing that matters in underwriting, credit and disclosure:
outputs that survive the question "says who, measured how?"
