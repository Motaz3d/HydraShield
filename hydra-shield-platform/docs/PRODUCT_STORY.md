# HydraShield — Product Story

**Status:** canonical positioning reference. Every page, report, pitch and
message must be consistent with this document. If a claim cannot be traced
to a real source and labelled with the evidence vocabulary below, it does
not ship.

---

## 1. The promise

> **HydraShield is Climate Extreme Intelligence.**
>
> We bring together scientific evidence, Earth observation, official open
> data and historical events to understand environmental extremes, identify
> who and what is exposed, understand the economic implications, and
> identify practical resilience actions.

We do **not** claim to predict disasters. We compute screening-level
intelligence from real, documented datasets and we say exactly what kind of
evidence every statement rests on. "Unknown" and "unavailable" are
first-class answers.

## 2. The evidence vocabulary (part of the brand)

Every statement carries a **temporal class**:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OBSERVED | Measured now/recently by a real instrument or official feed |
| HISTORICAL | Documented past events or past conditions from archives |
| FORECAST | Short-horizon model forecast from a real forecasting system |
| MODELLED | Computed from a model (incl. reanalysis), not directly measured |
| PROJECTED | Long-horizon scenario-conditional projection (e.g. IPCC AR6) |
| SCENARIO | A declared what-if computation, explicitly not a prediction |
| INFERRED | Derived indirectly from other evidence |
| UNKNOWN | Not knowable from available authoritative data |

…and a **claim status**: OBSERVED · DOCUMENTED · REPORTED · MODELLED ·
INFERRED · UNKNOWN. Historical evidence stays structurally separate from
modelled interpretation. Projections are never mixed with current
observations. Causes are DOCUMENTED or UNKNOWN — never invented.

## 3. Six hazards, one architecture

Wildfire, flood, drought, extreme heat, extreme wind and coastal/sea
exposure are **equal members** of one Climate Extreme Intelligence
architecture. Wildfire is our deepest pipeline (fire danger, fuel moisture,
spread context, historical fire events, smoke) — it is the proof of depth,
not the boundary of the product. No page may read as "a wildfire app with
add-ons".

Each hazard answers the same questions from real data: current conditions,
historical events where authoritative datasets exist, exposure, economic
meaning, and fitted solutions — each with its own sources, resolution,
freshness and limitations.

## 4. The intelligence chain

```
ENVIRONMENTAL EXTREME
  → EVIDENCE          (observed / historical / modelled, provenance-attached)
  → EXPOSURE          (who and what is in the way)
  → ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE  (structured exposure profile; honest non-quantification)
  → FINANCIAL CONSEQUENCE (what it means for finance & insurance, where a basis exists)
  → BUSINESS / GOVERNMENT NEED
  → SOLUTION          (nature-based, engineering, technology — with limitations)
  → RESILIENCE INVESTMENT
```

The platform never skips a link: no economic figure without exposure, no
exposure claim without evidence, no solution without stated limitations.

## 5. Who and what is exposed

HydraShield explains **who is exposed**, not only what the hazard is:

- **Population & settlements** — counts and concentration from WorldPop
  gridded estimates; urban areas and population centres
- **Critical facilities** — hospitals, schools, fire stations (OpenStreetMap
  via ohsome/Overpass, completeness caveated)
- **Cities & infrastructure** — built-up areas, transport nodes and networks
- **Businesses & sectors** — commercial and industrial areas, ports,
  logistics, tourism, agriculture, energy assets (from mapped land use,
  clearly labelled by completeness)
- **Agriculture** — cropland exposure (ESA WorldCover)

Exposure classes are reported separately — population exposure, business
exposure, infrastructure exposure, critical-service exposure — wherever the
data permits the distinction.

## 6. Two identities, one evidence base

**Scientific HydraShield** — evidence, data, satellite, historical events,
methodology, validation, provenance. Speaks to researchers, analysts,
agencies, and anyone who audits us.

**Commercial HydraShield** — risk, exposure, business, finance, insurance,
solutions, monitoring, alerts, resilience investment. Speaks to insurers,
real estate, construction, banking, investors, asset managers, energy,
logistics, ports, agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, telecoms, data
centers, infrastructure operators, municipalities and governments.

Both identities draw on the same underlying evidence and the same honesty
rules. The commercial story may never outrun the scientific one.

## 7. The product ladder (conversion through value)

FREE VALUE → ACCOUNT → MONITORING → ALERTS → SMS → PROFESSIONAL →
BUSINESS/GOVERNMENT.

- **Free:** point analysis for all six hazards, map layers, historical
  events, exposure, economic profiles, solutions, PDF reports — genuinely
  useful, rate-limited per IP.
- **Registered (free account):** save locations, analysis history, alert
  rules, email alerts, SMS after phone verification.
- **Professional/subscriber:** more rules, SMS + email, alert history, API
  key, full reports.
- **Business/government:** many monitored locations, organization users,
  multiple recipients, webhooks, API, reporting.

"Subscribe to monitor this" appears at the moment of value — never as a
wall in front of the free core.

## 8. What we never say

- "predicts disasters" / "predict and prevent" / "high accuracy" claims
- "prevents fires" / "guarantees protection" / "most accurate"
- market sizes, losses, or trends without an authoritative, dated source
- causes of events unless DOCUMENTED; outcomes unless DOCUMENTED
- capabilities of datasets we have not integrated (candidate sources are
  labelled candidate)

What we say instead: real data, official sources, Earth observation,
historical evidence, transparent uncertainty, traceable methodology,
screening indicators labelled as not validated.

## 9. One-paragraph versions

**Homepage hero (short):** HydraShield brings together the best available
evidence — Earth observation, official open data and historical events — to
understand environmental extremes, who and what they affect, what they mean
economically, and the actions that reduce exposure.

**Investor/partner (medium):** HydraShield is a Climate Extreme
