## Slide 2: The Problem
* **The Challenge:** Wildfires are becoming more frequent, severe, and unpredictable due to climate change.
* **The Gap:** Current wildfire risk models are often static, rely on outdated vegetation maps, and fail to account for dynamic soil-to-fuel moisture transfer.
* **The Consequence:** Civil protection agencies and municipalities lack real-time, predictive intelligence to optimize water-constrained interventions and dynamically plan protection zones.

## Slide 3: The Solution (HydraShield)
* **What it is:** An AI-driven Digital Twin for wildfire risk mitigation.
* **How it works:** We integrate Copernicus satellite data (Sentinel-1/2/3) with climate models and soil physics to predict dynamic moisture transfer.
* **The Result:** Transitioning from static risk mapping to real-time, predictive fire-spread modelling and actionable decision support.

## Slide 4: Underlying Technology & Innovation
* **Earth Observation (EO):** Utilizing Copernicus multispectral and synthetic aperture radar (SAR) data.
* **Scientific Modelling:** Combining soil physics, hydrology, and fire-spread algorithms.
* **Digital Twin:** A simulated environment that allows for scenario testing and dynamic protection-zone optimization.
* **Why Sofia Tech Park:** We require HPC (BRAIN++) for complex modeling and Laboratory Complex resources for scientific validation.

## Slide 5: Market Opportunity
* **Target Audience (B2G/B2B):** Municipalities, civil protection agencies, infrastructure operators, and eventually insurers.
* **Market Drivers:** Increasing regulatory requirements for climate adaptation and disaster resilience; growing financial losses from unmitigated wildfires.
* **Positioning:** A specialized DeepTech European ClimateTech platform, not just a generic SaaS.

## Slide 6: Business Model
* **Revenue Stream:** B2G / B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS).
* **Offerings:** 
  * AI-driven wildfire-risk intelligence
  * Scenario modelling & API data services
  * Integration with local operational systems (e.g., civil protection dashboards)
* **Phased Rollout:** Starting with digital validation, moving to municipal pilot testing, and scaling to European commercialization.

## Slide 7: Traction & Roadmap
* **Stage 1 (Current):** Digital Feasibility & Company Formation (Luxembourg HQ).
* **Stage 2 (Next):** Field Validation – testing algorithms against real-world fuel moisture dynamics.
* **Stage 3:** European Demonstration – participating in Horizon Europe/Interreg collaborative projects.
* **Stage 4:** Commercial Scale-up.

## Slide 8: Competitive Advantage
* **Scientific Rigor:** Focus on dynamic soil-to-fuel moisture transfer, a critical missing link in existing models.
* **European Integration:** Built on EU assets (Copernicus) and aligned with EU disaster resilience goals.
