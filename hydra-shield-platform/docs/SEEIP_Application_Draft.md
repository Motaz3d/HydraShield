## 3. Brief Description of your idea (Max 800 characters)
**Draft (796 characters):**
HydraShield is a ClimateTech and Earth Observation (EO) venture developing an AI-driven Digital Twin to mitigate wildfire risk. We integrate Copernicus satellite data (Sentinel 1/2/3), climate models, and soil physics to predict dynamic soil-to-fuel moisture transfer. This allows us to move beyond static risk maps to real-time, predictive fire-spread modelling and water-constrained intervention planning. We are applying to SEEIP to leverage Sofia Tech Park’s BRAIN++ HPC infrastructure and Laboratory Complex for the scientific validation of our algorithms and prototypes. By combining our Luxembourg-based commercial strategy with Bulgaria’s DeepTech R&D ecosystem, we aim to provide municipalities and civil protection agencies with actionable, evidence-based disaster-resilience tools.
