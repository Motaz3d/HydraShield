# Digital Feasibility & Company Formation

## Overview

Establishing Talaix in a strategic location selected based on clear criteria including regulatory environment, tax incentives, access to funding markets, and digital infrastructure. There is no pre-commitment to a specific country; rather, available options are evaluated and the most suitable one is chosen to achieve growth and funding objectives.

---

## Country Selection Criteria

Candidate countries are evaluated against the following criteria:

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Regulatory Environment** | Ease of company incorporation, speed of procedures, and transparency of commercial laws |
| **Tax Incentives** | Competitive tax regimes for startups and technology companies |
| **Access to Funding** | Availability of grant programs, accelerators, and business incubators (local and international) |
| **Digital Infrastructure** | Quality of connectivity, data centers, and the technology ecosystem |
| **Market Access** | Proximity to target markets (Europe, Middle East, Latin America) |
| **Political & Economic Stability** | Safe and stable environment for long-term growth |

---

## Candidate Countries & Regions for Incorporation

### European Options
- **Luxembourg**: Advanced regulatory environment, tax incentives for tech companies, access to CASSINI and Copernicus programs
- **Estonia**: e-Residency program, fully digital environment, competitive taxation
- **Netherlands**: Strong tech ecosystem, R&D incentives (WBSO)
- **Ireland**: Low corporate taxes, access to the EU market
- **Slovenia**: Low operating costs, government support for innovation

### Non-European Options
- **United Arab Emirates**: Free zones, zero tax, access to Middle East and Asian markets
- **Saudi Arabia**: Vision 2030, strong support for tech startups
- **Chile**: Start-Up Chile program, access to Latin American markets
- **Singapore**: Global tech hub, advanced regulatory environment

## Funding Strategy

### Financial Target
Secure initial funding of **€50,000 – €100,000** through a combination of:

1. **European Union Grants**:
   - **Copernicus Incubation Programme**: Support for startups using Earth observation data
   - **CASSINI**: Acceleration program for space and geospatial data companies
   - **Horizon Europe / EIC Accelerator**: Grants for high-impact innovations

2. **Local Programs in the Country of Incorporation**:
   - Government grants and incentives for tech startups
   - Local incubator and accelerator programs

3. **Complementary Funding**:
   - Angel investors
   - Crowdfunding platforms
   - Strategic partnerships with industry players

---

## Proof of Concept: Wildfire Digital Twin

### Objective
Build a Digital Twin model that simulates historical wildfire behavior using **Hindcasting** (re-simulating past events) to validate the accuracy of the predictive model.

### Methodology
1. **Data Collection**: Historical fire data from reliable sources (Copernicus, NASA FIRMS, local datasets)
2. **Re-simulation**: Run the model on documented past fire events
3. **Validation**: Compare model outputs with actual fire data
4. **Optimization**: Fine-tune model parameters based on validation results

### Expected Outcomes
- Proven reliability of the predictive model
- Publishable scientific documentation
- Solid foundation for attracting funding and partnerships
